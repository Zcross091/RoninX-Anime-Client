Everything you need to know about installation, safety, and updates.

Is RoninX Anime Client safe and free to use? +
Yes, RoninX is completely free, open-source, and contains zero ads, spyware, or paid tier paywalls. The full source code is hosted publicly on GitHub.
How do I install the APK on Android? +
Download the latest app-debug.apk file from our GitHub Releases page, tap on the notification to install, and allow 'Install from unknown sources' when prompted by Android settings.
Does it support casting to TV (Chromecast)? +
Yes, Media3 ExoPlayer includes native Google Cast support so you can beam 1080p anime directly to your Smart TV or Chromecast device.
How do I sync with my AniList account? +
Navigate to the Settings tab in the app, select 'Connect AniList', and authenticate via OAuth. Your watchlist and episode counts will update automatically while you stream.
Is RoninX the best free anime app for Android in 2026? +
RoninX is designed to be the fastest and most feature-rich free anime streaming and manga reading app available for Android. Built natively with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, it offers zero-lag 60FPS performance, AniList tracking, and 1080p streaming — all without any ads or subscription fees.
How do I watch anime without ads on my phone? +
Simply download the RoninX Anime Client APK from our GitHub Releases page. The entire app is ad-free by design — no popups, no banners, no video ads. Just pure, uninterrupted anime and manga.
Can I read manga offline with RoninX? +
RoninX includes a pre-fetch engine that caches manga pages as you read. While full offline download is coming in a future update, the cache system means previously read chapters load instantly without internet.
