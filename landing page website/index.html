Good questions

Before you
download.

Is RoninX free and open source?+

Yes. RoninX is designed as a free community project, with its source available on GitHub.

What devices does it support?+

The app is written for Android 8.0 and later. Check the latest release notes for device-specific compatibility.

Can I use AniList with RoninX?+

Yes. Connect your AniList account in the app to sync progress, favorites, and planning lists.

Does the manga reader support different layouts?+

Yes. The experience is designed for both vertical webtoon reading and traditional page-by-page layouts.
